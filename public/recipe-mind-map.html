Acros

Mono Soul 2 references
Intent
A high-contrast monochrome choice for daily life, texture and expressive portraits. The creator’s Fujifilm story values tonal depth and silver-halide character; catalog DR400, Highlight +2 and Shadow +3 create strong tonal separation. Use shape and luminance rather than palette matching.
Subjects
street, portrait, architecture, still-life, night
Light to consider
daylight, artificial, night
Palette
neutral
Observed references
Dark working interior: a person, side light and surfaces separated by luminance; color is not the story.
Caution
Color is discarded; avoid this when colorful clothing or festival color carries the story. The published creator story specifies ACROS+G while the saved catalog says Acros; keep the saved source unchanged and do not claim exact green-filter parity.
Preview assumptions
Use the saved film simulation, WB and tone settings for an approximate sRGB preview. Color is discarded; avoid this when colorful clothing or festival color carries the story. Nostalgic Negative

Cool Air · Scotland 1 references
Intent
A dramatic mist-and-hills interpretation using Nostalgic Negative’s amber highlight foundation with R -1 / B -3, Color +4 and strong Chrome. Highlight +1.5 and Shadow +3 create pronounced tonal separation; Clarity -3 softens texture without undoing that contrast.
Subjects
landscape, forest, coast, snow
Light to consider
overcast, fog
Palette
blue, cyan, neutral, orange
Observed references
Same valley: blue/cyan upper slopes and mist contrast with brown foreground; very deep hill shadows.
Caution
The Cool Air name is not a globally cool WB: B -3 reduces blue and Nostalgic Negative has amber highlights. Shadow +3 can lose dark hill detail; high highlight contrast may make bright snow harsh despite DR400.
Preview assumptions
Use the saved film simulation, WB and tone settings for an approximate sRGB preview. The Cool Air name is not a globally cool WB: B -3 reduces blue and Nostalgic Negative has amber highlights. Shadow +3 can lose dark hill detail; high highlight contrast may make bright snow harsh despite DR400.
Evidence
settings-inference · Reference appearance is observed; suitability and causal rendering remain interpretations. No paired untreated images or calibrated camera response.

Related direction

Plain Light · Scotland — Cool Air adds stronger tonal drama; Plain Light keeps a more restrained everyday balance.

Pro Neg. Hi

Plain Light · Scotland 1 references
Intent
The closest restrained everyday alternative in the Scotland set. Pro Neg. Hi, Auto WB with a small R +1 / B -1 shift, Color +1 and grain Off support believable skin and landscape color; DR200 / Highlight -0.5 modestly protect light detail while Shadow +1 adds form.
Subjects
portrait, landscape, street, architecture, animals, food
Light to consider
overcast, daylight, mixed
Palette
neutral, green, blue, orange
Observed references
Same valley: gray mist, brown moor and restrained color with readable pale house; less amber than the warm variants.
Caution
This is an inference from settings and the catalog’s grey-mist sample pairing, not a promise of calibrated color. Pro Neg. Hi and Shadow +1 still add contrast; it is not identical to Pro Neg. Std or a flat grading profile.
Preview assumptions
